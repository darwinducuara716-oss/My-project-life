👋 Welcome to My World

Hello and welcome! I'm Darwin, and this is my story — where I come from, the people I love, the challenges I've faced, and the dreams I hold close to my heart. Thank you for being here ❤️

📍 Where It All Began

I was born on November 10, 2009, in Cartagena del Chairá, Caquetá, Colombia, around 5 in the morning. My mother is Doris Diaz and my father is Fernando Ducuara.


We started our journey in a small rural village called Santo Domingo. Life was simple but difficult there, so we moved to Cartagena del Chairá, where we could build a more stable life. A few years later, we lived in Florencia for three years because of my mom's work. When we returned, we received a small plot of land, and together with my family, we built our home — a place where I felt truly happy.

📚 My Journey Through School

My first steps in education were at "Rayitos de Sol" Children's Home. Then I went to preschool and most of my primary years at Atanasio Girardot School. I studied at different campuses — Agro, Primero de Agosto — and later at José María Córdoba, where I am now finishing my studies.


The pandemic was a hard time for me. My grades struggled, but I never gave up. I learned that even when things are difficult, you keep moving forward 💪

💛 The People Who Matter Most

Life hasn't always been easy. My parents separated, and there were times when we had very little. But through every hard moment, my family has been my greatest strength.


My brother Deivy — he has been like a father to me when we needed one. My sister Lorena — always there with love and support. And my mom, my rock. They live in different places now, but they are always in my heart. Family isn't about being together physically — it's about loving each other, no matter the distance 🌍❤️

🌟 What I Love & My Dreams

🔴 🏃 📖 ✈️

I love the color red, I enjoy running, playing, watching videos, and learning something new every single day.


My dream was to study Criminology, but opportunities are limited where I live. So I have set new goals: I plan to study Law or Public Accounting. But my biggest dream of all? To build a beautiful family — a loving wife and a daughter I can cherish — and simply live a happy, peaceful life. That is my greatest ambition ✨

🚀 Ready for the Future

I may not have had everything easy, but I have love, hope, and a dream.

Here I come — ready to work, ready to learn, and ready to make my dreams come true.
